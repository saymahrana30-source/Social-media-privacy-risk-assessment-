Social Media Privacy Risk Assessment Framework

Answer a short questionnaire about your settings and habits and get a 0-100 privacy risk score, category-wise analysis, prioritised recommendations and a "what if I improve?" simulation. Nothing is scraped, no real profiles are examined, and you never type a phone number, address or password.

Start Privacy AssessmentTry the fictional demo profileView dashboard

Higher score = higher assessed exposure/risk. This is an educational risk framework, not a guarantee that an account will or will not be compromised.

10 categories

Profile, personal info, location, content, connections, tagging, account security, third-party apps, social engineering and digital footprint.

56 questions

YES / NO / SOMETIMES / NOT SURE or PUBLIC / FRIENDS / PRIVATE. Answers are processed in memory and never stored.

Privacy by design

Only scores and finding types are saved, for a limited time, and you can delete them with a one-time token.
